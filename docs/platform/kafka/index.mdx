Kafka-specific internals and operations. Tool-agnostic messaging concepts (queues vs. topics, delivery guarantees,
outbox) live in [Messaging Communication](/docs/architecture/microservices/messaging-brokers).

## Topics to cover
- Architecture: brokers, topics, partitions, replication, ISR, KRaft vs. ZooKeeper
- Producers: keys and partitioning, `acks`, idempotence, batching and compression
- Consumers: consumer groups, offset management, rebalancing, lag
- Delivery semantics: at-most-once, at-least-once, exactly-once (transactions)
- Ordering guarantees and retention / log compaction
- Kafka Streams and Kafka Connect
- Schema Registry and schema evolution
- Spring Kafka: listeners, error handling, retries, dead-letter topics
- Operations: sizing partitions, monitoring, common production issues
